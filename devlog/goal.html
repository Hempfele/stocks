Fantasy Portfolio
Goal Decisions
Play

Goal

Not about the money. Build a portfolio that feels right: ten areas of life, one company for each, chosen for the future you want to support.

  • Values over analytics — the choice shouldn't be based on price charts; it's about the companies you want to support and the life you want to see.
  • Self-portrait, not prediction — ten picks should say something about a person, not just their appetite for returns.

Gameplay

  • Two players to start. More can come later, but the first version is intimate and easy to understand.
  • Ten life areas. Each area is modeled after the hierarchy of needs, from nourishment to dreams and frontier.
  • One company per area. Picking locks the live price and virtually buys €1,000 of that stock.
  • Shared board. Every player sees every other player's picks; the fun is in comparing worldviews.
  • Season format. Picks lock during a season, values evolve over time, and there is a winner at the end.
  • Fair scoring. Each pick is worth €1,000 × current / locked price, so mixed currencies compare cleanly.

Requirements

  • Lightweight feel. No finance-app energy: numbers stay understated, no charts, no jargon. Picks and their stories come first.
  • No build step. Vanilla ES modules; the repo deploys to GitHub Pages as-is.
  • Demo mode first. Empty js/config.js runs the full game on fake data.
  • Locked means locked. Picks are insert-only and written server-side.
